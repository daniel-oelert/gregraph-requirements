# Effective change request status comes from the baseline branch

## Statement
The tool shall derive a change request's effective status from its sidecar on the baseline branch and show the working-tree status as proposed when the two differ.

## Rationale
What is decided on the baseline branch is what counts; a pending local status must not masquerade as decided.
