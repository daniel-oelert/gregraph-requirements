## Objective
Verify that `gregraph status` reports the WIP state and lists drafts and unbumped modifications.

## Preconditions
A repository containing one draft document and one document with unbumped modifications.

## Steps
Run `gregraph status`.

## Expected result
The header reports `WIP: yes` and the draft and modified documents are listed.
